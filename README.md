I turn slow, manual work into simple bots and agents that run on their own.

## 🛠 What I've built
- **HR Assistant Agent**: a Claude-powered Telegram agent that handles day-to-day HR for a 40-person team. Runs 24/7 on Railway.
  - **Attendance:** every evening it reads Face ID check-ins from Verifix, applies a 10-minute grace rule and each employee's own shift times, and drafts a report of late, late with a valid reason, absent with notice, and missed check-ins. HR approves or corrects it in plain words before anything is posted, and every result is logged to a Google Sheets tracker that calculates deductions automatically.
  - **Employee self-service:** employees press Start, HR confirms who they are, and each person can see their own late days, missed check-ins, charges and bonuses, or ask questions in their own words. Salary reports are coming next.
  - **Reasons and approvals:** employees send reasons for being late or absent to the bot, and HR approves or rejects them with one tap. An approved reason means that day isn't charged.
  - **Performance records:** in the managers' group it silently reads messages and records every charge and bonus by category, building a long-term record for each employee that HR pulls up with /record for salary reviews.
  - **Team engagement:** shift reminders with a new motivational quote every shift.
- **AI Recruiting Agent**: a Claude-powered Telegram bot that runs first-round hiring end to end. It chats with applicants in Uzbek, Russian or English and reads CVs (PDF, Word or photos). It screens them against each role's rules for 6 roles, auto-rejects or sends edge cases to HR with Invite/Reject buttons, and books Zoom interviews with a 10-per-night cap. It sends reminders at 60 and 10 minutes, alerts HR to same-day bookings, and logs every candidate to a Google Sheets pipeline. It also sends a daily summary and takes HR instructions by reply. Runs 24/7 on Railway.
- **Meta Ads Agent**: a Claude-powered agent that runs Facebook and Instagram lead ads for hiring CDL-A truck drivers. It builds full campaigns through the Meta Marketing API (campaign, lead form, ad set and ads) from videos sent to a Telegram bot, and keeps everything paused until HR taps "Go live". Every morning it reviews performance and sends a report with spend, leads, cost per lead and the best and worst video. It pauses losing ads on its own and asks for approval with Approve/Reject buttons before raising a budget or turning anything on. Hard limits in code cap budgets and how fast they can grow. Runs 24/7 on Railway.
- **Attendance Bot**: a Telegram bot that turns Face ID check-in data from Verifix into a daily attendance report for a 40-person team. It applies a 10-minute grace rule and each employee's own shift times, and splits the report into late, late with a valid reason, absent with notice, and missed check-ins. HR reviews the draft before it posts. Every Sunday it sends a weekly summary that flags anyone who missed more than their one day off. It sends shift reminders with a quote of the day at 01:00, 09:00 and 18:00, and logs every day to a Google Sheets payroll tracker that calculates deductions automatically. It also catches check-ins saved to duplicate or dismissed employee records. Runs on Google Apps Script.
- **Payroll Bot**: a Telegram bot that sends each of about 40 employees a private monthly salary report. It reads the monthly payroll Google Sheet and builds each person's report: fixed salary, bonuses and charges with the reason for each, and net pay in both USD and UZS. Employees join by pressing Start and sending their full name, and HR checks each match before anyone's account is linked to a salary. HR sees a preview of every report and has to type YES before anything is sent. Reports can go out one team at a time, for example Fleet/HR first and Dispatch once their numbers are final. Every report has ✅ Confirm and ❌ Something's wrong buttons, and each answer is saved with the employee's comment. Employees who write before their report is ready get an automatic reply.
- **Reels Agent**: a Claude-powered Telegram bot that publishes Instagram Reels for channel. HR sends a finished video to the bot. Claude looks at frames from the video and writes the hook, caption, hashtags and a cover-text idea, choosing the style that fits each video. Nothing is posted until the owner taps Approve, and the preview also has Rewrite, Edit, Post now and Delete buttons. Approved Reels are posted through the Instagram Graph API on a Mon/Wed/Fri 7 pm schedule and shared to Stories as well. If a Reel isn't approved in time, it moves to the next slot, and the bot sends a midday reminder on posting days. A local Telegram Bot API server lets it accept videos up to 2 GB. The code has a full test suite.
- **YouTube Growth Agent** *(in progress)*: a Claude-powered agent for YouTube content. It reviews scripts and sends the editor notes on hooks and pacing. It analyzes each finished video and writes SEO titles, a description with chapter timestamps and tags. It also picks 3 Shorts/Reels with exact cut times and a hook for each. It pulls YouTube Studio analytics through the vidIQ API, and a scheduled weekly report shows which videos are working and why. Next: daily Shorts posting from an approved queue.
