## 🛠 What I've built
- **AI Recruiting Agent**: a Claude-powered Telegram bot that runs first-round hiring end to end. It chats with applicants in Uzbek, Russian or English and reads CVs (PDF, Word or photos). It screens them against each role's rules for 6 roles, auto-rejects or sends edge cases to HR with Invite/Reject buttons, and books Zoom interviews with a 10-per-night cap. It sends reminders at 60 and 10 minutes, alerts HR to same-day bookings, and logs every candidate to a Google Sheets pipeline. It also sends a daily summary and takes HR instructions by reply. Runs 24/7 on Railway.
- **Attendance Bot**: a Telegram bot that turns Face ID check-in data from Verifix into a daily attendance report for a 45-person team. It applies a 10-minute grace rule and each employee's own shift times, and splits the report into late, late with a valid reason, absent with notice, and missed check-ins. HR reviews the draft before it posts. Every Sunday it sends a weekly summary that flags anyone who missed more than their one day off. It sends shift reminders with a quote of the day at 01:00, 09:00 and 18:00, and logs every day to a Google Sheets payroll tracker that calculates deductions automatically. It also catches check-ins saved to duplicate or dismissed employee records. Runs on Google Apps Script.
- **Payroll Bot**: automates payroll calculations step by step.
- **Reels Agent**: an AI agent for creating social media content.
